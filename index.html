WHY I BUILT THIS

A practical project for real entry-level security thinking.

SignalShield turns common help desk and cybersecurity incidents into short decision-based exercises. The goal is not to memorize definitions. It is to practice prioritization: what to verify, what to isolate, what evidence to preserve, and when to escalate.
